In person or remote

  • In person: a half-day workshop, with its break-out the same afternoon or on another day.
  • Remote: each workshop runs as two shorter sessions, and break-outs use breakout rooms with a facilitator in each.
  • Small groups: about six to eight people per facilitator, each organisation in its own group.
  • Built in our environment: nothing to install and no access needed to your systems.
  • Sample data only: no customer, patient or confidential data in any build.
