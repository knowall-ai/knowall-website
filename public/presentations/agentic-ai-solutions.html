Memory that consolidates while they sleep

A living Neo4j knowledge graph of people, places, companies and projects

Concepts, not keywords

People, places, companies and projects — and the relationships between them. Agents remember how things connect, not isolated facts.

They sleep on it

Overnight, each agent revisits the day’s conversations and looks for what relates to what it already knew — the same way sleep consolidates human memory.

Refined and strengthened

Memories are reorganised, duplicates merged and important links reinforced, so recall gets sharper over time instead of noisier.

New connections surface

Links nobody made explicitly — this person moved to that company, this decision affects that project — emerge from the graph itself.
