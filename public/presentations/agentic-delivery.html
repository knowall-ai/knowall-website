Open-source — the best of Agile and DevOps

T-Minus-15 is our open-source methodology for delivering projects, seamlessly blending the best principles of Agile and DevOps. At its heart we prioritise collaboration and transparency — daily stand-ups welcome our clients, ensuring swift issue resolution and alignment with project objectives.

A structured approach following Prep > Design > Engineer > Test > Operate keeps delivery efficient and effective — and we take pride in delivering to production at the earliest opportunity, gathering feedback from stakeholders and end-users in an iterative loop that consistently exceeds expectations.
