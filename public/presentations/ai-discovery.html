What it is

A focused, one-day engagement

The AI Discovery is a focused, one-day engagement designed to uncover how automation and AI can enhance your organisation. We work with your team to identify processes ripe for automation, gather ideas, and evaluate opportunities by value, effort and complexity — setting the stage for future AI projects.

Identify opportunities

Pinpoint the processes across your business that are ripe for automation and AI, gathered through surveys and on-site discussion.

Evaluate & prioritise

Assess each opportunity by value, effort and complexity, mapping them against your strategic objectives.

Understand AI

Learn the real capabilities, risks and limitations of AI — plus what other customers are doing — for a realistic picture.
