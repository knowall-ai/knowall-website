How it works

Attack. Defend. Fix. Report. Repeat.

1

Pennie attacks

With your written authorisation, Pennie probes your apps, APIs and cloud, and checks every new CVE against your code to prove what is really exploitable.

2

Ollie defends

Did Ollie see it? He watches your Microsoft Defender for Cloud and Sentinel around the clock, and every attack he missed becomes a new detection.

3

Ollie fixes

He opens a pull request with the fix and a test that proves it. Your developers review and merge, then Pennie attacks again to confirm it holds.

4

Poppie reports

Poppie scopes the work, tracks every finding as a risk, issue or task, and shows your score, time to detect and time to fix each month.
